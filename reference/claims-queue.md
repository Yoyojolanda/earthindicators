# Claims queue

Claims heard in the wild, waiting to be answered on claims.html. Built in batches of about 10 (or sooner on request).
Answers are written to apply generally: no local details (a state, a single glacier, a named person) unless the
claim can't be answered without them. Covered claims get extra search words (data-also) instead of a new answer.

  February 2026. No year in the next 100 years will be below 1.5 °C." Source chart: quadratic fit of monthly ERA5
  since 1980 vs 1850-1900.
  Shown by data (ERA5, site's +0.88 offset, as of 2026-10-12):
  - Same data, different fits, different dates: linear from 1970 -> 2033; quadratic from 1970 -> 2028; quadratic from
    1980 (the source chart, reproduced) -> spring 2026, 2.0 °C in 2037.
  - 2025 annual 1.47; Jan-Jun 2026 monthly 1.39-1.44; 12-month mean 1.42 in May 2026; 5 of 8 months since Feb 2026
    below 1.5. Annual values scatter about 0.10 °C around the fitted curve (1980-2025).
  Estimates (say so on the page; they rest on assumptions):
  - Where the long-term trend is now: depends on the fit; "around 1.5 °C" is defensible, an exact month is not.
  - The 100-year prediction, counted from 2026-10-12 (calendar years 2027-2126): if warming follows the source curve
    and years keep scattering as in the past, the chance that no year falls below 1.5 is roughly 45-50%; volcanoes,
    rolling 12-month windows or a multi-dataset average lower it. So: not supported by the data, but not shown wrong
    either; it will very likely be settled by about 2031. Do not call it wrong.
  - Paris 1.5 is defined as a 20-year average, so "passed" in that sense can only be confirmed years later.
  Link from too-late; maybe a small chart of annual values with the three fits.

- climate-optimum (heard 2026-10-12): "Enjoy your climate alarmism, while humanity enjoys our climate optimum." Answer:
  "climate optimum" (Holocene, Medieval) is an old name for past warm periods, not a finding that warmth is best;
  society is built for the stable climate of the last 10,000 years; what hurts is speed and going past that range
  (link crop-records, dinosaurs, medieval-warm, two-degrees); fewer cold deaths don't offset heat (link cold-deaths).
  Search words: optimum, climate optimum, warmer is better, beneficial warming, good for humanity.
- Additions: fire weather / longer fire seasons (Jolly et al. 2015) to wildfires; marine heatwaves roughly doubled
  since the 1980s (IPCC) to extreme-picks.
- Addition to too-late (heard 2026-10-17, Thomas Reis on X: "1.5 is physically impossible", aerosols mask ~0.5 °C so
  "we already live in a near 2 degree world", "smart money assumes 3 °C", "Earth is getting darker"). Mostly covered
  by too-late; no new answer. Add: (1) one line that Earth reflects less sunlight than in the early 2000s, measured by
  CERES (link eei.html, absorbed-sunlight chart), and that whether warming is speeding up is still debated; (2) give
  the aerosol masking as an estimate with its range (IPCC AR6: about 0.4 °C, range 0.0-0.8), not as a measured number;
  (3) keep "1.5 can no longer be avoided" (UN) rather than "physically impossible": getting back below it later would
  need large-scale CO2 removal. Search words: 3 degrees, 3 °C world, near 2 degrees, aerosol masking, hidden warming,
  darker Earth, albedo, stress test, on track, 1.5 impossible.
