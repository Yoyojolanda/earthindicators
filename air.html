Interpretation

    Daily mean surface air temperature (2 m above the ground or sea) averaged over the whole globe, area-weighted; each day is the mean of all 24 hourly values (UTC). Anomaly = daily temperature minus the 1991–2020 daily mean. "Above pre-industrial" adds Copernicus's standard 0.88 °C offset (1850–1900 to 1991–2020) to the 12-month average anomaly; Copernicus considers that offset accurate for 12-month averages, not for single days. Values to February 2024 are the series published by Copernicus; later days are computed here from ERA5 in the Copernicus Climate Data Store using the same method, and the newest days are preliminary. Generated using Copernicus Climate Change Service information; ERA5 (Hersbach et al.), cds.climate.copernicus.eu.
