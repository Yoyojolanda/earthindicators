Interpretation

    Daily means are averages of the hourly measurements that pass NOAA's quality checks; days without enough valid hours are missing and are shown as gaps, not filled in. Monthly means use months with at least 10 valid days; the trend is a centered 12-month running mean of those. Annual growth compares each month with the same month a year earlier and averages the differences. Recent values are preliminary and may be revised. Data: NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory, Mauna Loa daily mean CO₂ (gml.noaa.gov/ccgg/trends). Dr. Xin Lan, NOAA/GML, and Dr. Ralph Keeling, Scripps Institution of Oceanography.
